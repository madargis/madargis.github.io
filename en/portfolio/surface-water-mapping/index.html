Monthly water area of the King Fahd Dam reservoir, Bisha, from Sentinel-2: January 2017 to November 2026

A series of 107 usable dates shows the reservoir mostly between 500 and 850 ha, with a median of 669 ha and a largest extent of 846 ha on 26 February 2025. Six dates were flagged as detection failures and stay visible in the table.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

The problem

A water body or consultant needs a multi-year record of how a dam lake's water area changed, with no continuous ground measurement available to them.

The data

Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance, tile 38QKG, bands B03, B08, B11 and the SCL layer at 20 m. The clearest scene of each month from January 2017 to November 2026 (119 scenes, identifiers in water_area_series.csv), read as windows of cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs through the Earth Search STAC API (Element 84, collection sentinel-2-l2a) on 20 November 2026. Attribution as the Copernicus Sentinel legal notice requires: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2017-2026. Dam location 19.6963° N, 42.4874° E as published.

The method

  1. For each month: the least cloudy complete scene (tile cloud under 20%), reading only a 12 × 15.5 km window around the lake.
  2. Masked SCL classes no data, defective, cloud shadow, cloud and cirrus. A date is used when 98% of the reservoir zone is clear, which dropped 6 dates.
  3. Water = NDWI (B03 and B08) above 0, or B11 reflectance below 0.05. The first rule alone fails when the atmospheric correction clips water reflectance to zero, the second alone fails under summer sun glint, which it did on 41 dates.
  4. Reservoir zone: the largest connected area that is water in at least 5% of clear observations, grown by 100 m (1,485 ha). Areas are counted inside it only.
  5. Quality control: a date is flagged when its area is under half the median of the two dates before and the two after, since such a one-month drop is not physically possible for a lake this size.
  6. Repeated the count with NDWI thresholds −0.05 and +0.05 to measure sensitivity.

The result

Of 119 months: 107 usable dates, 6 flagged as detection failures and 6 dropped for cloud. Median water area 669 ha (697 and 637 ha at NDWI thresholds −0.05 and +0.05). Largest extent 846 ha on 26 February 2025, latest value 691 ha on 14 October 2026. The B11 rule alone would have missed half the water or more on 41 dates. The smallest accepted value, 325 ha on 7 February 2022, shows dark water outside the mask on its map, so it is a partial failure the check missed, and the true minimum is not known from this series.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. The results were not compared with finer imagery or gauge readings, so there is no accuracy figure. Automatic detection fails on some dates (sun glint, clipped atmospheric correction), and the quality flag catches sharp drops only. At 20 m, narrow arms and shores are measured roughly. Area is not volume without a level-storage curve, and it does not replace water-level gauging or a dam safety assessment.
