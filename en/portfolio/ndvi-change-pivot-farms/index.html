Centre-pivot vegetation change in Tabarjal, Al-Jawf: March 2019 to March 2026

Two Sentinel-2 scenes seven years apart show active vegetation (NDVI ≥ 0.3) in a 15 × 15 km window falling from 11,901 ha to 9,541 ha. 2,565 ha became active and 4,925 ha went inactive between the two dates.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

The problem

An agency wants to know how much the irrigated area of a centre-pivot district changed between two seasons, and where, without a field visit and with a stated accuracy.

The data

Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance, 10 m, tile 37RDP: scene S2A_37RDP_20190306_0_L2A (6 March 2019, processing baseline 02.11, tile cloud 0.08%) and scene S2B_37RDP_20260304_0_L2A (4 March 2026, processing baseline 05.12, tile cloud 0.005%). Read as windows of cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs through the Earth Search STAC API (Element 84, collection sentinel-2-l2a) on 20 November 2026. Licence: free, full and open access under the Copernicus Sentinel data legal notice. Attribution: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2019, 2026.

The method

  1. Searched Earth Search for scenes with tile cloud cover below 5% in the same season (10 February to 20 March) of 2019 and 2026, and picked 6 March 2019 and 4 March 2026, both on relative orbit 35.
  2. Read only the 15 × 15 km window (EPSG:32637, x 430000 to 445000, y 3320000 to 3335000) of the red band B04, the near-infrared band B08 and the scene classification layer SCL.
  3. Reflectance offset: the 2019 scene (baseline 02.11) has none. The 2026 scene (baseline 05.12) carries the +1000 offset in the ESA product; its Earth Search item says earthsearch:boa_offset_applied = true, yet the asset metadata still lists offset = -0.1. A pixel comparison with the same product in collection sentinel-2-c1-l2a gave a difference of exactly 1000, so the offset was already removed and nothing was subtracted.
  4. Masked SCL classes no data, defective, cloud shadow, cloud and cirrus (0.001% of the window in 2019, 0% in 2026).
  5. Computed NDVI = (B08 − B04) / (B08 + B04) for both dates, and change = NDVI 2026 − NDVI 2019.
  6. Active vegetation = NDVI ≥ 0.3. Gain = active in 2026 only, loss = active in 2019 only. Area = pixel count × 0.01 ha.
  7. Repeated the count at thresholds 0.2 and 0.4 and after a 30 m opening filter to measure how sensitive the result is.

The result

Active vegetation: 11,901 ha on 6 March 2019 (52.9% of the 22,500 ha window) and 9,541 ha on 4 March 2026 (42.4%). Gain 2,565 ha, loss 4,925 ha, net −2,360 ha (−19.8%), with 6,976 ha active on both dates. Mean NDVI of the window fell from 0.418 to 0.369. At thresholds 0.2 and 0.4 the net change is −2,902 ha and −2,293 ha.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. NDVI is an indirect measure, not a field measurement, and no reference data was available, so no error matrix was computed and the result is exploratory. One scene per year captures the crop state on that day: a field that is harvested or fallow on 4 March 2026 counts as loss even if it is still farmed, so the figures do not measure abandoned land, yield or water use. Pixel size is 10 m, and 4% of the loss area and 5% of the gain area lie in strips narrower than 30 m along field edges, where co-registration between the scenes (about one pixel) matters. The scenes come from Sentinel-2A and Sentinel-2B, whose bands differ slightly.
