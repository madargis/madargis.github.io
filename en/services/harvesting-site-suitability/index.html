GIS Support for Small-Dam, Water-Harvesting and Recharge Site Selection

Candidate sites for small dams, ponds and subsurface dams ranked by multi-criteria analysis, with first area and storage curves, for the study engineer.

Quoted per scope

Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

A single basin has dozens of possible narrows, and the engineer needs a ranked shortlist before site visits, survey and boreholes.

Who it is for

Engineering offices commissioned for harvesting and dam studies, water authorities and development organisations, and large farms.

What you receive

  • Criteria layers: catchment area, valley narrowness, slope, geology, distance to users.
  • A ranked list of candidate sites with coordinates.
  • A first level-area-storage curve per site from the elevation model.
  • Maps in the office's template.
  • A method and limits note.

What you supply

  • The basin or area boundary.
  • The criteria and weights from the engineer.
  • A topographic survey, if any.

How the work goes

  1. The office fills in the request form, with the area and engineer's name.
  2. We agree the criteria, weights and structure type.
  3. We prepare the layers, identify narrows and rank the sites.
  4. We compute first storage curves and review the list with the engineer.
  5. We deliver the layers, list and maps. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Storage curves come from a 30 m model with vertical accuracy under 4 m, so they compare sites and are not for design. The ranking does not assess dam foundations, seepage or runoff volume. The output is not a dam study and does not replace survey, boreholes or a hydrology study.

Regulated work

The dam study, design and safety are prepared by a qualified engineering office, and harvesting structures need a licence from the environment, water and agriculture ministry. Madar ranks sites and prepares layers on criteria approved by the engineer.

Out of scope

  • Hydrology studies and runoff calculation
  • Structural design and dam safety
  • Boreholes and field survey
  • Licence applications
  • Signing
