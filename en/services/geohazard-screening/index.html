GIS Support for Geohazard Assessment: Sinkholes, Landslides and Rockfall

An inventory of features visible in imagery, factor layers and preliminary susceptibility maps, prepared for a geotechnical engineer or engineering geologist who assesses the site and signs.

Quoted per scope

Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

Before boreholes and field work the engineer needs a spatial picture of the site: where the pits, cracks, steep slopes and likely rockfall paths are.

Who it is for

Geotechnical and engineering offices, developers in mountainous or karst areas, and road contractors.

What you receive

  • An inventory of visible pits, cracks and slides as GeoPackage, with image dates.
  • Slope, aspect, geology and drainage layers.
  • A preliminary susceptibility map with weights approved by the engineer.
  • Report maps in the office's template.

What you supply

  • The site and project boundary.
  • Topographic survey and borehole results, if any.
  • The responsible engineer's name.

How the work goes

  1. The office fills in the request form, with the site, hazard type and engineer's name.
  2. We agree the factors, sources and weights with the engineer.
  3. We map the features from imagery and prepare the factor layers.
  4. We produce the maps and review them with the engineer.
  5. We deliver the layers, maps and method note. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

The inventory covers only what is visible in available imagery; subsurface cavities do not show. The open 30 m elevation model is not enough for site slope analysis. The susceptibility map is a desk screening, not a hazard assessment and not a substitute for boreholes and site investigation.

Regulated work

A geohazard assessment and site investigation are prepared and signed by a geotechnical engineer or licensed office, under the Saudi Building Code and the competent authority. Madar supplies layers, inventories and maps only.

Out of scope

  • Boreholes and testing
  • Geotechnical assessment and remediation design
  • Signing
  • Field work
