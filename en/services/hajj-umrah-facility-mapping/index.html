Hajj and Umrah Facility and Route Mapping

Maps and databases of the facilities, camps and routes of pilgrim service companies, with walking catchments and simple dashboards.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

Camp, facility and assembly-point maps drawn by hand or spread across files, and hard to update each season.

Who it is for

Domestic and international pilgrim service companies, Makkah and Madinah hotel operators, and Umrah tour organisers.

What you receive

  • A facility and camp database with the required attributes.
  • Bilingual print maps.
  • Walking catchments and routes between sites.
  • A team web map, if requested.

What you supply

  • The client's approved camp and facility plans.
  • The site list.
  • The languages required.

How the work goes

  1. We agree content and languages.
  2. We map facilities from the client's plans.
  3. We compute distances and catchments.
  4. We deliver maps and database by an agreed date before the season.

Accuracy and limits statement

Maps come from the client's plans and open data. It is not crowd analysis, flow simulation or a safety plan. Aerial imagery of the holy sites is used only under a licence the client holds.

Out of scope

  • Crowd simulation and density analysis
  • Approved safety and evacuation plans
  • Aerial imaging
