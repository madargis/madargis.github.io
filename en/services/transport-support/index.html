GIS Support for Transport Studies

Maps, layers and tidy count tables for the engineer of a transport or traffic impact study at an accredited office, to the receiving authority's specification.

From USD 150Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

The study guideline demands maps and files: an aerial map of roads, junctions and parking, layouts over aerial imagery, and count tables. The engineer wants them ready to the authority's specification.

Who it is for

Consulting offices accredited for traffic and traffic impact studies. The service is for offices, not individuals.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • An aerial map of surrounding roads: lane count, road type, speed limit.
  • Layers for junctions and control type, parking and no-parking zones, public transport and taxi stops, and the future projects the engineer specifies.
  • The access layout placed over aerial imagery, from the engineer's drawing.
  • Traffic count tables arranged from the office's data.
  • Shapefiles in the required reference.

What you supply

  • Study area boundary and study level.
  • Raw count data.
  • The access drawing.
  • Road attributes collected in the field.
  • The map template.
  • The required coordinate reference.

How the work goes

  1. The office fills in the request form, with the location, study level, receiving authority and the responsible engineer's name.
  2. We go through the guideline's list of outputs with the engineer and agree what we prepare.
  3. We send a one-page offer, priced per hour or as a package.
  4. We build the road and junction layer from an open source, then correct it with the engineer's field data.
  5. We add parking, public transport stops and future projects.
  6. We arrange the count tables and reconcile their totals with the raw files.
  7. We produce the maps in the office's template and export the files in the required reference.
  8. The engineer reviews the outputs and one revision round follows.
  9. We deliver the maps, layers, tables and a sources note. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Geospatial support outputs for the study engineer, not a traffic study. Road attributes come from open sources and from data supplied by the office, and the engineer verifies them. Count tables rearrange the office's data without altering it. Any accessibility analysis is a GIS analysis, not a capacity analysis.

Regulated work

A traffic impact study is prepared by an accredited office and a responsible transport and traffic engineer. Trip generation, capacity analysis, simulation, conclusions and signing stay with the engineer.

Out of scope

  • Field traffic counts
  • Trip generation
  • Capacity analysis and simulation
  • Designing improvements
  • Signing
  • Submission to the authority
