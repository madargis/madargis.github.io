Urban Planning and Real Estate

Land suitability, site selection, parcel screening and urban growth.

Services in this category

How it is delivered
  • Multi-Criteria Land Suitability Analysis Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Ranking land by suitability for a given use with criteria and weights agreed with the client, including a sensitivity test.
  • Preliminary Land Parcel Screening Report Delivered by MadarFrom USD 15 A 3 to 5 page report before purchase: location and surroundings, slope, distance to the nearest drainage line, and change on the land since 2016.
  • Urban Growth and Density Analysis Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Measuring urban expansion over decades, its direction and density, in time-series maps and tables for planning studies.
  • GIS Support for Plans and Planning Studies Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Existing-conditions analysis and land-use and services maps for a planning office preparing a plan or study, which its accredited planner signs.
  • Heritage and Tourism Mapping and Site Inventory Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope A spatial inventory of heritage and tourism sites, their services and routes, as a database and print or interactive maps.
  • Hajj and Umrah Facility and Route Mapping Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Maps and databases of the facilities, camps and routes of pilgrim service companies, with walking catchments and simple dashboards.
