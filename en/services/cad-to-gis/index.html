CAD to GIS Conversion and Coordinate Transformation

Converting DWG and DXF drawings into a GIS database with a defined coordinate reference and a layer mapping table.

From USD 150

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

CAD drawings with no coordinate reference, or an old one. Unclassified layers that will not load into the owner's database. A handover rejected for that reason.

Who it is for

Contractors who hand over as-built drawings in the owner's data model, engineering and survey offices, and organisations moving their data between datums.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • A File Geodatabase or GeoPackage in the required schema.
  • A mapping table between CAD layers and GIS feature classes.
  • A conversion report: source and target reference, transformation method and EPSG code, and control point residuals.
  • A check report from the QA/QC service.
  • The original file, untouched.

What you supply

  • DWG or DXF files.
  • The owner's data model or the delivery specification.
  • The coordinate reference if known, or shared control points.
  • What each layer means.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the number of files, the required reference and the specification.
  2. We inspect a sample file: does it have a reference, are layers named consistently, do blocks and text carry attributes?
  3. We send a one-page offer with a draft mapping table for you to approve.
  4. We clean the CAD: remove duplicates, close polygons, separate layers.
  5. We georeference or transform between datums with a documented method, then measure residuals at the control points.
  6. We convert to GIS feature classes and carry attributes over from text and blocks.
  7. We run the QA/QC checks on the result.
  8. We review the result visually over open aerial imagery.
  9. We deliver the database, the mapping table, the conversion report and the check report. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

A desk conversion. The accuracy of the result follows the accuracy of the original drawing and of the control points supplied by the client. The published transformation between Ain el Abd and KSA-GRF17 is accurate to 0.1 m, so the result does not suit work that needs better accuracy without field points. The deliverable is not a land survey.

Out of scope

  • Field surveying
  • Official land surveys
  • Designing a full enterprise data model
  • BIM models
