Pipeline and Power-Line Corridor Screening

Proposing alternative corridors for a pipeline, power line or road by least-cost analysis, comparing length and obstacles.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

The need for documented route alternatives before survey and detailed studies.

Who it is for

Oil, gas and utility consultants at the route-study stage, and project developers.

What you receive

  • Proposed corridors and a cost surface.
  • A comparison table: length, crossings and constraints.
  • A criteria and weights note.

What you supply

  • Start and end points.
  • Constraints and criteria.
  • Project data, if any.

How the work goes

  1. We write criteria and weights with the engineer.
  2. We build the cost surface.
  3. We compute and compare corridors.
  4. We deliver maps and table.

Accuracy and limits statement

A desk screening from open and client data. It is not route design, a survey or a right-of-way approval. Decisions and design stay with the responsible engineer.

Out of scope

  • Engineering route design
  • Field survey
  • Right-of-way procedures
