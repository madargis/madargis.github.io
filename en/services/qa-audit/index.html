Spatial Data QA/QC and Accuracy Checks

An independent desk check of your data before handover: coordinate reference, geometry validity, overlaps, attribute completeness and positional accuracy.

From USD 150Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

A handover gets rejected or sent back. Parcel, address or point data that cannot be trusted. A team that spends its time preparing data instead of using it.

Who it is for

Contractors and consultants who hand data over to an owner with a specification, survey offices and development organisations. Also a land investor who wants parcel data verified.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • The cleaned data in its original format.
  • A PDF audit report in Arabic or English: coordinate reference, geometry validity, overlaps and gaps, duplicates, attribute completeness and domains, and positional accuracy where check points exist.
  • A numbered error layer you can open in your own software.
  • A log of every change, before and after.

What you supply

  • The data: File Geodatabase, GeoPackage, Shapefile or DWG.
  • The delivery specification or data model, if there is one.
  • Check points, if any.
  • What the data is for.
  • Who signs the final deliverable.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form: data type, format, feature count, specification, deadline, purpose.
  2. We ask for a sample, one layer or 5% of the data, and check it to estimate the effort.
  3. We send a one-page offer: the list of checks, deliverables, exclusions, price, duration and acceptance criterion.
  4. We receive the data through a channel you choose. The original is kept read-only and work happens on a second copy.
  5. We run the automated checks with a Python script, then review a sample of the results by eye.
  6. We classify errors as critical, important or cosmetic. We fix what is in scope and document what needs your decision without changing it.
  7. Where check points exist, we compute positional accuracy: RMSE, number of points and their distribution.
  8. We rerun the checks on the result and show the difference before and after.
  9. We deliver the data, the report, the error layer, the log and the accuracy and limits statement, with a 20-minute call to explain the results. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

A desk check of the data as received on the date of receipt. Positional accuracy is computed from check points supplied by the client, with their count and RMSE stated. Where there are no check points, no judgement is made on positional accuracy. The deliverable is not an official survey or a legal boundary, and it does not guarantee acceptance by the receiving authority.

Out of scope

  • Field work
  • Proof of ownership
  • Survey decisions and any signed official deliverable
  • Full re-digitising
  • Fixes that need the owner's decision
