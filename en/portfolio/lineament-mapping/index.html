Lineaments around Mahd adh Dhahab from four NASADEM hillshades

We extracted 355 lineaments, 289 km in total, from NASADEM hillshades lit from four directions over a 30 × 30 km window of the Arabian Shield. The main trend is north-east to south-west, between 40° and 50°.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Lineament Extraction and Density Mapping

The problem

A geologist or exploration team wants a first layer of lineaments with their density and orientation over a wide area, with a stated source and parameters, before structural interpretation and field work.

The data

NASADEM_HGT version 001 by NASA JPL (2020): 1 arc-second (about 30 m) elevations from the SRTM flight of February 2000, in whole metres above the EGM96 geoid, tiles n23e040 and n23e041. Only a window was read from Microsoft Planetary Computer (collection nasadem, no account) on 20 November 2026. Licence: openly shared without restriction under the NASA EOSDIS guidance. Citation: NASA JPL. (2020). NASADEM Merged DEM Global 1 arc second V001 [Data set]. NASA Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center. https://doi.org/10.5067/MEASURES/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001

The method

  1. Read a 30 × 30 km window (EPSG:32637, x 675015 to 705015, y 2585015 to 2615015) and reprojected it to 30 m cells; elevation runs from 921 to 1,305 m.
  2. Four hillshades lit from 0°, 45°, 90° and 135°, sun 30° high, vertical exaggeration 2.
  3. Canny edge detection (2-pixel smoothing, thresholds at the 80th and 90th percentiles of gradient), then a probabilistic Hough transform: minimum length 600 m, maximum gap 90 m.
  4. Merged the four directions from longest to shortest, dropping a line when more than half of it lies within 60 m of a kept line.
  5. Rose diagram of line length in 10° bins, and density as line length inside a 1 km radius divided by the circle's area.

The result

355 lineaments, 289 km in total (from 495 candidates before merging), mean length 813 m, longest 1,617 m. The 40° to 50° bin holds 16.8% of the length, and the length-weighted mean trend is 58°. Mean density is 0.32 km/km², peaking at 3.2 km/km² in the hill masses in the north of the window. Lines per light direction: 140 from 0°, 113 from 45°, 87 from 90° and 155 from 135°.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. Lineaments are linear terrain features, not confirmed faults: they include ridge edges, wadi floors, road cuts and pits, and structural interpretation stays with the geologist. Cells are 30 m and the minimum length is 600 m, so shorter lines and lines buried under sand on the plains are missed. The NASADEM user guide (NASA JPL, 2020) gives the SRTM requirement as 16 m absolute vertical error at 90% and a global average absolute height error of about 6.8 m. Results follow the light direction and parameters: under light from 0° and 135°, 63% and 74% of lines run within 30° of perpendicular to the light, against 33% for random orientations, yet the north-east trend dominates under all four lights. The lines were not compared with a geological map or field work, so no accuracy figure can be given.
