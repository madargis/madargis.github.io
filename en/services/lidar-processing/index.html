LiDAR Point-Cloud Processing

Classifying point clouds and deriving terrain, surface and building models from LiDAR data supplied by the client.

Quoted per scope

In preparation

This service is in preparation and cannot be requested yet.

The problem

A huge point cloud the team cannot classify or turn into a product.

Who it is for

Offices and contractors with airborne or terrestrial LiDAR, and overseas land investors asking for contours from open LiDAR.

What you receive

  • A classified LAS/LAZ cloud.
  • Terrain and surface models.
  • A classification quality report on a sample.

What you supply

  • LAS/LAZ files and their reference.
  • The products required.
  • Check points, if any.

How the work goes

  1. We check density, reference and coverage.
  2. We classify and derive models.
  3. We review a sample by hand.
  4. We deliver files and report.

Accuracy and limits statement

This service is in preparation: the founder has not yet classified LiDAR on a project. It opens after a documented demonstration is built.

Out of scope

  • LiDAR capture
  • Detailed architectural modelling
  • Official deliverables
