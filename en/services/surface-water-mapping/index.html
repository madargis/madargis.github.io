Surface Water, Reservoir and Flood Extent Mapping from Imagery

The area of dam lakes, ponds and post-flood water measured over time from open Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery.

From USD 150Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

An organisation needs a record of how water area changed in a lake or area, and no continuous ground measurement exists.

Who it is for

Dam operators, water authorities and their consultants, development organisations and researchers.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • A time series of water area with a chart.
  • Water extent maps for selected dates as GeoPackage.
  • A list of valid dates and those dropped for cloud.
  • A report with method, accuracy and code.

What you supply

  • The lake or area boundary.
  • The time period.
  • The level-storage curve, if any, to turn area into volume.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the site and period.
  2. We prepare the imagery and mask clouds and shadows.
  3. We compute water indices, set the threshold and check against higher-resolution imagery.
  4. We produce the series and maps and write the report.
  5. We deliver the layers, report and code. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Resolution is 10 m for Sentinel-2 and 30 m for Landsat; narrower water bodies are poorly measured. Cloud during floods often prevents optical imaging. Area is not volume without your storage curve. Results do not replace water-level gauging.

Out of scope

  • Area-to-volume conversion without a storage curve
  • Radar image analysis
  • Dam safety assessment
  • Field work
