GIS Support for Environmental Impact Assessments

Baseline maps and analyses for an environmental impact assessment, delivered to the qualified environmental consultant who prepares the study.

Quoted per scope

Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

The study needs dozens of maps and cover and sensitivity analyses, and the environmental team is busy with the content.

Who it is for

Environmental consultancies qualified with the country's environmental authority.

What you receive

  • Maps of site, surroundings, land cover and sensitive areas.
  • Distance and area tables.
  • Maps in the office template.

What you supply

  • Project boundary and components.
  • The office template.
  • Environmental field survey data, if any.

How the work goes

  1. We agree the map list with the consultant.
  2. We prepare layers and analyses.
  3. We produce maps in the template.
  4. The consultant reviews, includes and signs.

Accuracy and limits statement

Support outputs from open data and the office's data, not an impact assessment. Judgement on impacts and recommendations stays with the consultant.

Regulated work

An environmental impact assessment is prepared and submitted by an environmental consultant qualified with the National Center for Environmental Compliance in Saudi Arabia, or its equivalent elsewhere. Madar provides the geospatial support only; the signature belongs to a licensed office.

Out of scope

  • Writing the study or judging impacts
  • Environmental field survey
  • Submission to the authority

Tools

  • QGIS
  • Google Earth Engine
