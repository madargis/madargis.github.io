GNSS Office Post-Processing

Processing the client's raw GNSS files (static or PPK) into coordinates with a quality report.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

Raw field files that must be processed against a reference station and documented before use.

Who it is for

Small survey offices and contractors who collect GNSS data but lack the time or software to process it, and researchers.

What you receive

  • A coordinate table in the required reference.
  • A processing report: reference stations, observation time, fixed solutions and deviations.
  • A flag on every point below the agreed threshold.

What you supply

  • RINEX or raw files with antenna height and type.
  • Reference station data or access to it.
  • The required reference and accuracy.

How the work goes

  1. We check the files: duration, satellites, gaps.
  2. We process against the reference station and compute solutions.
  3. We review quality and flag weak points.
  4. We deliver the table and report.

Accuracy and limits statement

Accuracy follows field observation quality, the reference station and observation time. The coordinates are not an official survey deliverable; using them in a survey decision needs a licensed office's signature.

Out of scope

  • Field observation
  • Signing the deliverable
  • CORS network subscriptions
