What Madar is

Madar is a small digital office that delivers geospatial services remotely and builds tools that shorten everyday GIS work. It is led by a GIS specialist with a BSc in Geomatics and Surveying Engineering and the PMP certification. Every deliverable comes with an accuracy and limits statement. Every demo in the portfolio is built from open data and labelled as a demo. For hydrology studies, traffic impact studies and official survey deliverables we provide spatial support only; a licensed office signs. There are no client logos on this site, no testimonials and no project counts.
