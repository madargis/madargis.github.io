Digitizing and Vectorization

Capturing features from aerial or satellite imagery or scanned plans into GIS layers, with attributes and a written capture standard.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

The information sits in an image or a paper plan, and there is no layer that can be analysed.

Who it is for

Organisations and offices that need a building, road or parcel layer that does not exist, and researchers who need a layer for a study area.

What you receive

  • The digitized layers with the required attributes.
  • A capture standard: scale, minimum feature size and drawing rules.
  • A check report: topology and completeness on a sample.

What you supply

  • The images or plans and the right to use them.
  • The features and attributes required.
  • The coordinate reference.

How the work goes

  1. We agree the features and capture standard on a small sample.
  2. We digitize and fill attributes.
  3. We check topology and completeness and review a sample by eye.
  4. We deliver the layers and report. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Digitizing accuracy follows the accuracy and scale of the image or plan. The output is not a survey and not a legal boundary.

Out of scope

  • Buying imagery
  • Field verification
  • Official property boundaries
