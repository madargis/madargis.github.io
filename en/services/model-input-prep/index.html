Input Preparation for Hydraulic and Groundwater Models

Terrain, roughness layers and mesh for a HEC-RAS 2D model, and grids and layers for a MODFLOW model, prepared for an engineer who builds and runs the model.

Quoted per scope

In preparation

This service is in preparation and cannot be requested yet.

The problem

Half of modelling time is spatial preparation, and the engineer wants clean, documented inputs.

Who it is for

Hydraulic and groundwater engineers and their offices.

What you receive

  • A terrain surface merged with the survey.
  • A roughness layer from land cover.
  • A first computational mesh and boundary lines.
  • A method note.

What you supply

  • The model boundary and survey.
  • The engineer's parameter table.
  • The responsible engineer's name.

How the work goes

  1. The office fills in the request form.
  2. We agree sources and parameters with the engineer.
  3. We prepare and check the inputs.
  4. We deliver the files and the note.

Accuracy and limits statement

In preparation: Madar has no documented practice yet in HEC-RAS or MODFLOW. When available: limited to spatial inputs, with no running, calibration or interpretation of results.

Out of scope

  • Running and calibrating the model
  • Interpreting results
  • Signing

Tools

  • HEC-RAS RAS Mapper
  • QGIS
  • FloPy (planned)
