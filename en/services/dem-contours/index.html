Elevation Models, Contours and Profiles

Preparing an elevation model from open sources or the client's data: clipping, merging, contours, slope and long sections.

Quoted per scopeDelivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

A quick picture of the terrain is needed before a field survey is commissioned.

Who it is for

Consultants and offices at the early study stage, researchers, and land investors who want a feel for the terrain.

What you receive

  • The clipped elevation model as GeoTIFF.
  • Contours, slope and aspect.
  • Long sections for requested routes.
  • A source and accuracy statement.

What you supply

  • The area boundary.
  • Private elevation data, if any.
  • Contour interval and reference.

How the work goes

  1. We choose the best source and state its accuracy.
  2. We prepare the model and derive layers.
  3. We review the result against current imagery.
  4. We deliver files and the accuracy statement.

Accuracy and limits statement

Copernicus DEM is 30 m with a stated vertical error under 4 m, and it is a surface model that includes buildings. Contours from it are for exploration, not design. It does not replace a field survey.

Out of scope

  • Field survey
  • Design
  • Buying commercial models

Tools

Demos from the portfolio

  • Contours, a profile and cut-and-fill for a hypothetical platform on a hillside near Mahd adh Dhahab, from NASADEM
