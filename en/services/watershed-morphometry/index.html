Watershed Delineation and Morphometric Analysis

Catchment, wadi network and stream orders mapped with morphometric parameters, for researchers, farms and organisations that need a first picture, not a signed study.

From USD 150

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

An organisation needs to know the catchment that drains to its site and its characteristics, before deciding whether it needs a full study.

Who it is for

Researchers and graduate students, development organisations, and farm and project owners at the idea stage.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • Catchment and sub-catchment boundaries as GeoPackage.
  • The wadi network with Strahler orders.
  • A morphometric table: area, perimeter, relief, drainage density, form factor, longest flow path.
  • Maps in your template.
  • A method and accuracy note.

What you supply

  • The outlet location or area boundary.
  • The purpose of the analysis.
  • A topographic survey, if any.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the outlet and purpose.
  2. We prepare the elevation model and fill sinks.
  3. We derive flow, catchments and network and compare them with recent imagery.
  4. We compute the parameters and produce the maps.
  5. We deliver the layers, table and note. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Catchments are derived from the Copernicus DEM at 30 m with a stated vertical accuracy under 4 m, which weakens in cities and flat plains. The parameters describe shape; they are not discharges or flood levels. The output is not a hydrology study and is not for plan approval.

Out of scope

  • Discharge and design-storm calculation
  • Hydrological and hydraulic modelling
  • Signed hydrology studies
  • Field surveying
