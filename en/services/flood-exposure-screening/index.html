Flood Exposure Screening for Land and Sites

A desk check of a parcel or project: whether it lies in a wadi course or depression, how high it sits above the nearest flow path, and whether water appeared there in past floods.

From USD 150Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

A buyer wants to know early whether land is exposed to floods, to decide whether a hydrology study is needed before buying.

Who it is for

Investors and landowners, developers at the purchase stage, and offices screening many sites before a study.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • A site map with wadi courses and the contributing catchment.
  • A height-above-nearest-drainage layer.
  • Historical water extents visible in imagery, with dates.
  • A relative exposure class per parcel: low, medium, high, with the reason.
  • A two-page note with the result, the limits and a study recommendation where needed.

What you supply

  • The land or parcel boundaries.
  • The date of any known flood in the area.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the land boundary.
  2. We map wadis and catchment and compute height above the nearest flow path.
  3. We search Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery for water after known floods.
  4. We classify exposure and write the note.
  5. We deliver the maps and the note. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

The screening uses a 30 m elevation model and 10 to 30 m imagery and computes no discharges, depths or return periods. Absence of water in imagery does not mean a site is safe. A flood screening is not a flood study and is not submitted to any authority for plan or building approval.

Out of scope

  • Hydrology studies
  • Discharge and depth calculation
  • Protection works design
  • Field surveying
