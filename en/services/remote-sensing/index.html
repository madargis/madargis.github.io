Remote Sensing Analysis

Analysis of open Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery for water, agriculture and change detection, answering one defined question: what changed on the ground, and when.

From USD 150

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

An organisation needs to know what changed on the ground and when, in vegetation, surface water, irrigation or urban growth, without a field visit and with a stated accuracy.

Who it is for

Development organisations and their contractors, environmental consultants, and researchers who need an analysis for a study.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • An analysis report of 5 to 10 pages: question, data, method, results, limits.
  • Maps and time series of indices such as NDVI and water indices.
  • Result layers as GeoTIFF and GeoPackage.
  • An accuracy assessment with an error matrix where reference data exists.
  • Documented code, so the analysis can be rerun.

What you supply

  • The study area boundary.
  • The time period.
  • The question to be answered.
  • Any field or reference data.
  • The report format you need.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the question, area, period and purpose.
  2. We check image availability: coverage, cloud and the spatial resolution the question needs.
  3. We send a one-page offer stating what the imagery can and cannot answer.
  4. We prepare the data: collection choice, cloud masking, temporal compositing.
  5. We compute the indices, the classification or the change detection.
  6. We validate against reference data or higher-resolution imagery and compute the accuracy.
  7. We produce the maps and charts and write the report.
  8. We review the outputs against the checklist.
  9. We deliver the report, the layers and the code, with an explanation call. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

Results are derived from Sentinel-2 imagery at 10 m or Landsat at 30 m on the stated dates. Indices are indirect measurements, not field measurements. Results must not be used to estimate crop yield or water volume without field data. Classification accuracy is as stated in the report; without reference data the result is exploratory.

Out of scope

  • Field work
  • Buying commercial imagery
  • Signed hydrology studies
  • A hosted monitoring platform
  • Subscription farm monitoring
