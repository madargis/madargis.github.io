Drive time to hospitals in Buraydah: who is inside 10 and 20 minutes, and who is not

On the OpenStreetMap road network with WorldPop population estimates, 72.7% of the estimated population is within 10 minutes' drive of the nearest mapped hospital and 95.3% within 20 minutes, at fixed free-flow speeds.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Demo date2026-11-20

Related serviceHealth and Emergency Service Coverage

The problem

Health and emergency planners need to know who falls outside an acceptable travel time, and where a new site would close the largest gap.

The data

OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20: the drivable road network and hospitals (amenity=hospital or healthcare=hospital) in the study box plus a 0.08° (about 8 km) margin. Licence ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Population: WorldPop 2026 estimates at 100 m (R2025A, DOI 10.5258/SOTON/WP00839), licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 2026-11-20.

The method

  1. Study box 26.2 to 26.48 N and 43.83 to 44.12 E around Buraydah; network and hospitals come from a wider box so access is not cut at the edge.
  2. Directed network that respects one-way streets, with a fixed speed per road class (km/h): motorway 100, trunk 80, primary 60, secondary 50, tertiary 40, unclassified 30, residential 25; links 30 to 50, any other class 25. Mapped speed limits are not used.
  3. 21 hospitals after merging points within 150 m of each other, each joined to its nearest node.
  4. Time from each population cell to the nearest hospital with Dijkstra on the reversed network (home to hospital).
  5. Each cell is joined to its nearest node; cells more than 500 m from the network count as off network.
  6. Gap candidates: network points in 1 km squares holding people outside 10 minutes, then a greedy pick of three points that add the most people within 10 minutes.

The result

Estimated population in the box 465,202: within 10 min 338,411, 10 to 20 min 105,078, over 20 min 6,398, off network 15,316. Candidates would add within 10 min: C1: 29,413 people, C2: 21,495 people, C3: 12,975 people. Network 37,732 nodes and 98,891 links; hospitals with an emergency tag: 1.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. Not an approved emergency plan and not an assessment of hospital capacity or specialties. Times use fixed free-flow speeds with no congestion or signals, so they lean low and real coverage is lower. The hospital list comes from OpenStreetMap and may miss hospitals or include facilities that are not hospitals; which ones take emergencies is unknown. WorldPop is a modelled 100 m estimate, not a census. Service roads and farm tracks are not in the network, so some edge populations show as off network. Candidates are network points; land, ownership and zoning are not checked.
