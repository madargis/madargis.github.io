Cadastral Back-Office Support

Desk preparation of parcel files inside the engineering office's workflow: polygon, coordinate table, area comparison and overlap detection.

Priced by volumeDelivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

Land registry batches open large numbers of parcels with fixed deadlines. The office needs deed-to-ground comparison, overlap detection, transformation to the national reference and documented accuracy, fast and without errors.

Who it is for

Licensed engineering offices working on survey reports and survey decisions. The service is for offices, not property owners.

What you receive

  • A file per parcel: the parcel polygon on the national reference, the coordinate table and the computed area.
  • A comparison of the deed area with the computed area, and the difference.
  • A report of overlaps and gaps with the neighbouring parcels supplied by the office.
  • A draft parcel plan in the office's template.
  • A notes log for every parcel that needs the engineer's decision.

What you supply

  • Field survey data: GNSS or total station.
  • The deed data and dimensions.
  • Neighbouring parcels, if any.
  • The plan template.
  • The office's acceptance rules.

How the work goes

  1. The office gets in touch through the request form and we send a sample parcel file built from demo data.
  2. One or two paid trial parcels at the hourly rate.
  3. A written agreement: price per parcel or hour, turnaround, confidentiality, and that signing and responsibility belong to the office.
  4. We receive the batch through the office's channel with the parcel list. All data stays the office's property and inside its channel.
  5. We enter the survey, build the polygon and transform it to the national reference with a documented method.
  6. We compare dimensions and area with the deed and detect overlaps.
  7. We fill in the parcel file and the notes log and run the checklist.
  8. We hand the batch to the office's engineer for review and correction.
  9. The batch closes once the engineer accepts it.

Accuracy and limits statement

Desk preparation of data supplied by the office. Accuracy follows the office's field survey. The output is a draft for the engineer's review; it is not a survey decision, a survey report, or proof of ownership or boundaries.

Regulated work

Survey decisions and survey reports are issued and signed by a licensed engineering office. The office owns the field survey, the decision, the signature and the relationship with the owner and the platform.

Out of scope

  • Field surveying
  • Issuing the survey decision or survey report
  • Signing
  • Dealing with the owner or official platforms
  • Settling boundary disputes
