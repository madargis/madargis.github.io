Vegetation and Afforestation Monitoring

Measuring vegetation change at planting sites, parks and rangelands over the years, with maps and tables.

From USD 150Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

The need for visual and numeric proof of planting progress or rangeland decline.

Who it is for

Planting contractors and operators who must report, environmental consultants and organisations.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • Change maps for each period.
  • A table of green area and change rates.
  • Before and after images for key sites.

What you supply

  • Site boundaries and planting dates.
  • The required period.

How the work goes

  1. We assemble the time series.
  2. We compute indices and thresholds.
  3. We review a sample on high-resolution imagery.
  4. We deliver maps and tables.

Accuracy and limits statement

Young seedlings do not show at 10 m in the first years. The result is a cover indicator, not a tree count or an official compliance report.

Out of scope

  • Tree counting
  • Field verification
  • Official compliance reports

Tools

  • Google Earth Engine
  • Sentinel-2
  • Landsat
