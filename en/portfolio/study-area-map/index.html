Study-area map for a thesis or report: Kingdom of Bahrain

A print-ready study-area map with main map, locator inset, scale bar, north arrow, graticule, legend and sources. Built from OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth by code that reproduces it.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Maps for Reports and Theses

The problem

Theses and reports need a study-area map with all map elements and cited sources. Many are sent back for a missing scale, projection or source.

The data

OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20: coastline, motorway, trunk and primary roads, governorate boundaries (admin level 4) and cities. Licence: ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Natural Earth 1:10m admin 0 countries, downloaded from naciscdn.org on 2026-11-20, public domain.

The method

  1. Download the layers in one cached request, and build land polygons from the coastline.
  2. Project to WGS 84 / UTM 39N (EPSG:32639), which fits Bahrain's location.
  3. Add a 0.1° graticule, a 10 km scale bar and a north arrow.
  4. A Natural Earth locator inset shows Bahrain in the Gulf and the main map extent.
  5. A legend and a note on projection, sources and download date.

The result

A 1600 px PNG map. Land 730 km² in 6 coastline polygons, 420 km of motorway, trunk and primary roads, 4 governorates and 6 labelled cities. 10 km scale bar, 0.1° graticule.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. The map covers the main island group and leaves out the Hawar Islands in the south. Boundaries and coastline are community data and are not official boundaries. The land area follows the OpenStreetMap coastline, including reclaimed land drawn there. The locator inset is small scale and heavily generalised. Not an official survey map.
