Lithological and Alteration Mapping from Satellite Imagery

Analysis of ASTER, Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery to highlight rock units and alteration zones rich in iron oxides, clays and carbonates.

From USD 150Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

A team needs a fast screening of a large area before field work, to decide where to start sampling, without buying commercial imagery.

Who it is for

Exploration companies and their geologists, licence-round bidders, and geology researchers and graduate students.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • Colour composites, band ratios and mineral indices as GeoTIFF.
  • A map of candidate alteration zones as GeoPackage.
  • Maps in your template with scale, image source and date.
  • A report of 5 to 8 pages: data, method, results, limits.
  • Documented code, so the analysis can be rerun.

What you supply

  • The study area boundary.
  • The target mineralisation type.
  • The geological map and sample locations, if any.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the area, target and deadline.
  2. We check image availability, vegetation and shadow.
  3. We prepare the imagery and compute composites, ratios and indices.
  4. We compare results with the geological map and available samples and document differences.
  5. We deliver the layers, maps, report and code, with an explanation call. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

ASTER resolution is 15 to 30 m, Sentinel-2 10 to 20 m, Landsat 30 m. Indices point to surface mineral groups, not specific minerals, and are affected by sand cover and desert varnish. The map is a desk screening to guide field work, not a discovery and not a resource estimate; field verification by a geologist is required.

Out of scope

  • Signed geological interpretation
  • Field work and sampling
  • Hyperspectral image analysis
  • Resource estimation
