GIS Support for Plans and Planning Studies

Existing-conditions analysis and land-use and services maps for a planning office preparing a plan or study, which its accredited planner signs.

Quoted per scope

Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

The study needs spatial analysis and many maps, and the planning team is small.

Who it is for

Urban planning and engineering offices preparing subdivision plans or planning studies.

What you receive

  • Existing-conditions layers and maps.
  • Service accessibility analysis and rate tables.
  • Maps in the office template.

What you supply

  • Study boundary and the plan, if any.
  • The authority's requirements.
  • The office template.

How the work goes

  1. We agree analyses and maps with the planner.
  2. We prepare layers and analyses.
  3. We produce maps and tables.
  4. The planner reviews, includes and signs.

Accuracy and limits statement

Support outputs, not an approved plan or study. Planning decisions and signature stay with the accredited office.

Regulated work

A plan or planning study is prepared and signed by an engineering or planning office accredited with the municipality or authority. Madar provides the geospatial support only; the signature belongs to a licensed office.

Out of scope

  • Planning design and signature
  • Submission to the municipality
  • Traffic studies

Tools

  • ArcGIS Pro
  • QGIS
  • AutoCAD
