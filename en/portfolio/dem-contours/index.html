Contours, a profile and cut-and-fill for a hypothetical platform on a hillside near Mahd adh Dhahab, from NASADEM

From NASADEM we drew 10 m contours over a 3 × 3 km window and a 1 km profile, and computed cut and fill for a hypothetical 150 × 100 m platform on a 7.1° hillside. At the balanced level of 1,055.0 m the cut is 31,061 m³, equal to the fill.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Demo date2026-11-20

Related serviceElevation Models, Contours and Profiles

The problem

An engineer or developer at concept stage wants contours, a profile and a first earthworks estimate for a site not yet surveyed, to compare options before ordering a survey.

The data

NASADEM_HGT version 001 by NASA JPL (2020): 1 arc-second (about 30 m) elevations from the SRTM flight of February 2000, in whole metres above the EGM96 geoid, tile n23e040. Only a window was read from Microsoft Planetary Computer (collection nasadem, no account) on 20 November 2026. Licence: openly shared without restriction under the NASA EOSDIS guidance. Citation: NASA JPL. (2020). NASADEM Merged DEM Global 1 arc second V001 [Data set]. NASA Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center. https://doi.org/10.5067/MEASURES/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001

The method

  1. A 3 × 3 km window around 40.8074° E, 23.5114° N, about 7 km west of Mahd adh Dhahab (EPSG:32637), resampled bilinearly to 5 m and 30 m cells.
  2. Contours every 10 m with an index line every 50 m, from the 5 m grid.
  3. A hypothetical 150 × 100 m platform with its long side along the contours; the fall line under it from the mean gradient.
  4. Balanced level: the mean ground elevation inside the platform, so cut equals fill. Volume = sum of differences × cell area, on the 5 m grid and again on 30 m cells for comparison.
  5. A 1 km profile along the fall line through the platform centre.

The result

Elevation in the window runs from 1,014 to 1,181 m with a mean slope of 6.9°, drawn as 122 contour lines totalling 110 km. The ground under the platform slopes 7.1° towards the north-north-east (13°). At the balanced level of 1,055.0 m the cut is 31,061 m³ and the fill the same, with a deepest cut of 10.2 m and a deepest fill of 9.4 m. On 30 m cells the cut is 30,212 m³. Each metre of change in level moves 15,000 m³ between cut and fill. The profile drops 39 m over 1 km.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. The platform is hypothetical and for illustration only; the site was not chosen for any project. The NASADEM user guide (NASA JPL, 2020) gives the SRTM requirement as 16 m absolute vertical error at 90% and a global average absolute height error of about 6.8 m; the model is a radar surface of February 2000 in whole metres. The 5 m grid is interpolated from cells of about 30 m and adds no detail, and the platform covers only about 16 original cells. Each metre of DEM error at a fixed level moves 15,000 m³, about half the computed cut. No side slopes, bulking or shrinkage factors, topsoil or rock are included. The contours are for exploration, not design, and do not replace a field survey.
