Demos

  • Desk screening of hydrothermal-alteration indicators around Mahd adh Dhahab from Landsat 9

    We computed iron-oxide and clay band ratios from a clear Landsat 9 scene over a 30 × 30 km window around the Mahd adh Dhahab mine. High values of both ratios overlap on 0.7 km², and 19% of that lies within 2 km of the mine's two records.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • A 26-layer folder processed with five Madar Automation Toolkit commands

    We prepared 26 open layers in three formats and five coordinate systems and ran them through reproject, clip, convert, merge and area report. The five steps took 15.7 seconds on this machine.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Ranking candidate sites for a new cafe in Al Malqa, Riyadh

    We scored 176 sites on the district's main roads against four criteria with stated weights. The top site scores 0.71 of 1, with about 9,021 people (WorldPop estimate) within a 10-minute walk.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Urban expansion in Al Narjis district, Riyadh: October 2017 to October 2026

    Two Sentinel-2 scenes nine years apart show 1,128 ha of new urban fabric inside Al Narjis district (4,816 ha). In a sample of 100 detections where OpenStreetMap buildings are mapped, 50 had a building within 15 m.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • CAD to GIS and Ain el Abd to KSA-GRF17 transformation, a Riyadh block

    We read a layered DXF in Ain el Abd and rebuilt it as attributed GIS layers in a KSA-GRF17 GeoPackage. Relabelling the coordinates instead of transforming them moves every vertex by 75.7 m.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Contours, a profile and cut-and-fill for a hypothetical platform on a hillside near Mahd adh Dhahab, from NASADEM

    From NASADEM we drew 10 m contours over a 3 × 3 km window and a 1 km profile, and computed cut and fill for a hypothetical 150 × 100 m platform on a 7.1° hillside. At the balanced level of 1,055.0 m the cut is 31,061 m³, equal to the fill.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Desk screening of building exposure to wadi channels in Muhayil Asir

    We classed 2,902 OpenStreetMap buildings in Muhayil Asir by their height above and distance from the nearest wadi channel, using NASADEM. 71 buildings fall in the high class and 295 in the medium class.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • A municipal parcels and utilities data model in GeoPackage, with a data dictionary and integrity rules

    We designed 8 feature classes, 15 domains (10 coded lists) and 4 relationships, built them in a GeoPackage with triggers that reject out-of-domain values and missing references, and loaded an OpenStreetMap sample of a Riyadh neighbourhood.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Camps, facilities and routes in Mina as mapped in OpenStreetMap

    We mapped 566 camps, 52 health points and nearby services in Mina and measured walking along the network: 87% of camps are within 10 minutes of a mapped health point. The map shows what is mapped, not everything that exists.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Drive time to hospitals in Buraydah: who is inside 10 and 20 minutes, and who is not

    On the OpenStreetMap road network with WorldPop population estimates, 72.7% of the estimated population is within 10 minutes' drive of the nearest mapped hospital and 95.3% within 20 minutes, at fixed free-flow speeds.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • AlUla heritage and visitor map with a bilingual site list

    We merged 71 heritage and visitor sites from Wikidata and OpenStreetMap into one numbered list, 23 of them with both Arabic and English names, plus visitor services and a 59 km driving route linking five main sites.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Lineaments around Mahd adh Dhahab from four NASADEM hillshades

    We extracted 355 lineaments, 289 km in total, from NASADEM hillshades lit from four directions over a 30 × 30 km window of the Arabian Shield. The main trend is north-east to south-west, between 40° and 50°.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Walking catchments of six Blue Line stations, Riyadh Metro

    On the OpenStreetMap walking network we computed the area reachable on foot in 5, 10 and 15 minutes from six stations. Together the 15-minute catchment covers 8.37 km² and 261.3 km of streets.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Centre-pivot vegetation change in Tabarjal, Al-Jawf: March 2019 to March 2026

    Two Sentinel-2 scenes seven years apart show active vegetation (NDVI ≥ 0.3) in a 15 × 15 km window falling from 11,901 ha to 9,541 ha. 2,565 ha became active and 4,925 ha went inactive between the two dates.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Quality check of OpenStreetMap buildings, Jarir District, Riyadh

    We checked 2,285 OpenStreetMap building footprints in Jarir District (2.58 km²) with Madar Quality Checker. It found 8 overlaps and 17 footprints with no street name, and no invalid or duplicate geometry.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Land suitability for a small solar site east of Riyadh, three criteria with stated weights

    We combined NASADEM slope with distance to OpenStreetMap power lines and roads after excluding built-up land, farms and wadis. 19.5 km² rate very high, holding 7 contiguous sites of 20 ha or more.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Study-area map for a thesis or report: Kingdom of Bahrain

    A print-ready study-area map with main map, locator inset, scale bar, north arrow, graticule, legend and sources. Built from OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth by code that reproduces it.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Terrain and buildings block model in 3D, central Abha

    We built a 3D model of a 2 × 2 km window in central Abha from NASA's NASADEM and OpenStreetMap buildings, exported as one 1.7 MB GLB file. It holds 80,000 terrain triangles and 284 buildings extruded from the ground.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Terrain pre-processing for the upper Wadi Hanifa catchment at Al-Uyaynah, north-west of Riyadh

    From the 30 m NASADEM elevation model we derived flow direction, flow accumulation, the stream network and the catchment above an outlet near Al-Uyaynah. The catchment covers 414 km² and its longest flow path is 63.6 km.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Morphometry of the Wadi Na'man catchment east of Makkah from NASADEM

    We delineated the Wadi Na'man catchment above an outlet where it leaves the mountains, ordered its streams by Strahler and computed its shape parameters. It covers 661 km², reaches order 5 and has a drainage density of 0.82 km/km².

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.
