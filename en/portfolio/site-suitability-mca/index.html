Land suitability for a small solar site east of Riyadh, three criteria with stated weights

We combined NASADEM slope with distance to OpenStreetMap power lines and roads after excluding built-up land, farms and wadis. 19.5 km² rate very high, holding 7 contiguous sites of 20 ha or more.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Demo date2026-11-20

Related serviceMulti-Criteria Land Suitability Analysis

The problem

Picking land for a solar or logistics project across a wide area needs a documented, defensible method before any field study.

The data

Elevation: NASADEM at 1 arc second (about 30 m), tile n24e046, NASA JPL (2020), LP DAAC, DOI 10.5067/MEaSUREs/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001, public domain, downloaded on 2026-11-20 from OpenTopography (data services of the OpenTopography Facility, NSF awards 2410799, 2410800 and 2410801). Roads, power lines and exclusion areas from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20, licence ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The method

  1. Search area 24.55 to 24.75 N and 46.78 to 46.99 E (485 km²) on a 30 m grid in UTM 38N.
  2. Slope in percent from reprojected NASADEM, scored 1 at 0% and 0 at 10%.
  3. Distance to power lines: 1 at the line, 0 at 10 km. Distance to main paved roads: 1 at the road, 0 at 5 km.
  4. Exclusions: slope over 10%; built-up, industrial, farm, cemetery, quarry, military, airport and protected land; 100 m around buildings and wadis, 30 m around roads, 50 m around power lines and railways.
  5. Base weights: slope 0.40, power 0.40, roads 0.20; suitability is the weighted sum from 0 to 1.
  6. Sites: contiguous areas scoring 0.8 or more and covering 20 ha or more, ranked by mean score.
  7. Sensitivity: four alternative weight sets; we measure how much of the top 10% of cells stays and where site S1 ranks.

The result

Of 485 km², 414 km² (85%) is excluded, the largest part a mapped military area covering 214 km², and 57.9 km² for slope. Suitability: low 3.0, moderate 16.1, high 32.8, very high 19.5 km². Best sites: S1: 24 ha, mean 0.88, power line 0.6 km, road 0.2 km; S2: 22 ha, mean 0.87, power line 0.3 km, road 0.3 km; S3: 40 ha, mean 0.87, power line 0.3 km, road 0.2 km; S4: 23 ha, mean 0.87, power line 0.4 km, road 0.1 km; S5: 34 ha, mean 0.87, power line 0.6 km, road 0.2 km. Top 10% kept under other weights: equal 92%, slope-led 79%, grid-led 74%, road-led 74%.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. Not a feasibility study, a planning approval or a check of ownership, regulations or grid capacity. NASADEM is a 30 m radar surface from 2000 with noise and does not replace a survey. Distances are straight lines, not connection routes; a nearby line does not mean a connection is possible, and substations are not scored. OpenStreetMap may miss lines, buildings or recent land use. The result follows the chosen weights, which are a demo assumption.
