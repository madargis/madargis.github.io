Madar's services are arranged in categories that cover the fields of geospatial work. Madar delivers some directly, delivers others with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output, and is still preparing the rest.

  • Hydrology and Water Services: 15 Catchments and wadis, flood screening, groundwater and water networks.

  • Utilities, Energy and Telecom Services: 6 Utility network data, renewable-energy siting and line-of-sight analysis.
