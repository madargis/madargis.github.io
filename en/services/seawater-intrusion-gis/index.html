GIS Support for Coastal Seawater Intrusion Studies

Maps of coastal well salinity and its change over time, shoreline change and land use, prepared for the hydrogeologist who interprets and advises.

Quoted per scopeDelivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the output

Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.

Request this service

The problem

Salinity readings are spread across wells and years, and the hydrogeologist needs to see them in space and time together.

Who it is for

Hydrogeology and environmental offices, coastal water authorities and researchers.

What you receive

  • A database of coastal well salinity readings.
  • Salinity maps per period and difference maps between periods.
  • Shoreline and land-use change from Landsat.
  • Maps in the office's template.

What you supply

  • Well salinity or conductivity readings with coordinates, dates and depths.
  • The study area boundary.
  • The hydrogeologist's name.

How the work goes

  1. The office sends the data and the request form.
  2. We check the data and harmonise units and reference.
  3. We prepare the time maps and shoreline change.
  4. We deliver the layers and maps. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

The maps interpolate between wells, and their accuracy follows well density, distribution and reading depths. The service does not identify the cause of salinity or the position of the intrusion front at depth, and it is not an aquifer model.

Regulated work

Interpreting seawater intrusion, recommendations and coastal aquifer modelling belong to the hydrogeologist or a specialist office. Madar prepares the data and maps.

Out of scope

  • Hydrogeological interpretation
  • Numerical intrusion modelling
  • Sampling
  • Signing
