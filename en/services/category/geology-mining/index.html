Geology and Mining

Geological mapping and exploration analysis from imagery and spatial data.

Services in this category

How it is delivered
  • Mining and Quarry Licence Area Maps and Coordinates Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 The boundary of an exploration or quarry licence site prepared in the national reference, with the coordinate table and the maps for the application and periodic reports.
  • Lithological and Alteration Mapping from Satellite Imagery Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 Analysis of ASTER, Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery to highlight rock units and alteration zones rich in iron oxides, clays and carbonates.
  • Lineament Extraction and Density Mapping Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 Mapping the linear features visible in elevation models and imagery, with their density and orientation.
  • GIS Support for Mineral Prospectivity Mapping Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Alteration, lineament, geology and geophysics layers combined into a prospectivity map, with the model and weights set by the client's geologist.
  • Geological Map Digitizing and Geodatabases Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 Paper, scanned or CAD geological maps converted into an organised spatial database of units, contacts, faults and measurements.
  • Exploration Databases: Drillholes, Samples and Trenches Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Drilling, sample and trench data organised in one spatial database, with checks of coordinates, duplicates and completeness.
  • GIS Support for Resource Reports and Mining Feasibility Studies Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Maps, figures and plans for a resource report or feasibility study, prepared for the team of the qualified person who writes and signs it.
  • Quarry and Stockpile Volumes from Drone and Survey Data Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Drone imagery or survey points sent by the client processed to compute excavation and stockpile volumes and the change between two dates.
  • Mining Disturbance and Rehabilitation Monitoring from Imagery Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 The area of ground disturbed by a mine or quarry measured over the years, with rehabilitation work documented in maps and time series.
  • GIS Support for Geohazard Assessment: Sinkholes, Landslides and Rockfall Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope An inventory of features visible in imagery, factor layers and preliminary susceptibility maps, prepared for a geotechnical engineer or engineering geologist who assesses the site and signs.
  • Sand Dune Encroachment Monitoring for Roads and Facilities Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 The advance of sand and dunes towards a road or facility measured over the years from open imagery.
  • InSAR Ground Motion and Subsidence Monitoring In preparationQuoted per scope Ground subsidence or uplift measured in millimetres per year from Sentinel-1 radar time series.
  • Preparation and Mapping of Published Geophysical Data In preparationQuoted per scope Published airborne magnetic and radiometric grids turned into GIS layers and standard derivatives a geologist can use.
