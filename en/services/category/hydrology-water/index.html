Hydrology and Water

Catchments and wadis, flood screening, groundwater and water networks.

Services in this category

How it is delivered
  • GIS Support for Hydrology Studies Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputFrom USD 150 Elevation model, catchments, wadi flow paths and report maps prepared for the study engineer at a qualified office.
  • Watershed Delineation and Morphometric Analysis Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 Catchment, wadi network and stream orders mapped with morphometric parameters, for researchers, farms and organisations that need a first picture, not a signed study.
  • Runoff Input Layers: Land Cover, Soils and Curve Number Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Land-cover, hydrologic soil group, curve-number and impervious-surface layers prepared for the study engineer from the engineer's value table.
  • Flood Exposure Screening for Land and Sites Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 A desk check of a parcel or project: whether it lies in a wadi course or depression, how high it sits above the nearest flow path, and whether water appeared there in past floods.
  • Surface Water, Reservoir and Flood Extent Mapping from Imagery Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 The area of dam lakes, ponds and post-flood water measured over time from open Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery.
  • Input Preparation for Hydraulic and Groundwater Models In preparationQuoted per scope Terrain, roughness layers and mesh for a HEC-RAS 2D model, and grids and layers for a MODFLOW model, prepared for an engineer who builds and runs the model.
  • GIS Support for Groundwater Potential Mapping by Multi-Criteria Analysis Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Factor layers prepared and a multi-criteria analysis run to produce a groundwater potential zone map, with weights approved by a hydrogeologist.
  • Well and Aquifer Databases Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope One spatial database of wells, licences, water levels, production and water quality, with check rules, maps and a monitoring dashboard.
  • GIS Support for Well and Well-Field Siting Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Constraint, distance and restricted-zone maps and the available information around a proposed well, prepared for the hydrogeologist who prepares the study and chooses the site.
  • GIS Support for Small-Dam, Water-Harvesting and Recharge Site Selection Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Candidate sites for small dams, ponds and subsurface dams ranked by multi-criteria analysis, with first area and storage curves, for the study engineer.
  • Irrigated Area and Centre-Pivot Mapping Delivered by MadarFrom USD 150 An inventory of irrigated fields and active pivots and their area each season from Sentinel-2 and Landsat time series.
  • Profiles and Cut-and-Fill Quantities for Irrigation and Drainage Canals Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Survey data processed to produce the longitudinal profile, cross-sections and cut-and-fill quantities for an irrigation or drainage canal at the design level.
  • Soil Salinity, Sabkha and Surface Water Quality Screening from Imagery In preparationQuoted per scope First maps of salt-affected soils and sabkhas, and turbidity indices in lakes, calibrated with field measurements supplied by the client.
  • GIS Support for Coastal Seawater Intrusion Studies Delivered with a licensed office or specialist who signs the outputQuoted per scope Maps of coastal well salinity and its change over time, shoreline change and land use, prepared for the hydrogeologist who interprets and advises.
  • Water and Wastewater Network Databases Delivered by MadarQuoted per scope Building or cleaning a spatial database for a water or wastewater network: pipes, valves and connections, with topology and attribute checks.
