Portfolio

A demo built from open data, not a client project. All demos

  • Desk screening of hydrothermal-alteration indicators around Mahd adh Dhahab from Landsat 9

    We computed iron-oxide and clay band ratios from a clear Landsat 9 scene over a 30 × 30 km window around the Mahd adh Dhahab mine. High values of both ratios overlap on 0.7 km², and 19% of that lies within 2 km of the mine's two records.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • A 26-layer folder processed with five Madar Automation Toolkit commands

    We prepared 26 open layers in three formats and five coordinate systems and ran them through reproject, clip, convert, merge and area report. The five steps took 15.7 seconds on this machine.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Ranking candidate sites for a new cafe in Al Malqa, Riyadh

    We scored 176 sites on the district's main roads against four criteria with stated weights. The top site scores 0.71 of 1, with about 9,021 people (WorldPop estimate) within a 10-minute walk.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

  • Urban expansion in Al Narjis district, Riyadh: October 2017 to October 2026

    Two Sentinel-2 scenes nine years apart show 1,128 ha of new urban fabric inside Al Narjis district (4,816 ha). In a sample of 100 detections where OpenStreetMap buildings are mapped, 50 had a building within 15 m.

    A demo built from open data, not a client project.

What Madar is

Madar is a small digital office that delivers geospatial services remotely and builds tools that shorten everyday GIS work. It is led by a GIS specialist with a BSc in Geomatics and Surveying Engineering and the PMP certification. Every deliverable comes with an accuracy and limits statement. Every demo in the portfolio is built from open data and labelled as a demo. For hydrology studies, traffic impact studies and official survey deliverables we provide spatial support only; a licensed office signs. There are no client logos on this site, no testimonials and no project counts.
