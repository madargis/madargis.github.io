Web Maps and Dashboards

An interactive map or a dashboard with agreed indicators, published inside your account or on your hosting.

From USD 300

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

Data and results the decision maker never sees. What is needed is an interactive map or a dashboard with clear indicators that is easy to update.

Who it is for

Consultants, contractors and organisations that want to show their data and results to decision makers.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • A web map or dashboard published inside your ArcGIS Online account, or a static web map built with open libraries on your hosting.
  • Prepared, symbolised layers with pop-ups.
  • Agreed indicators and filters, 3 to 6 indicators.
  • A one-page update guide.
  • A handover session for whoever will run the dashboard.

What you supply

  • An ArcGIS Online account with publishing rights, or your own web hosting.
  • The data.
  • The list of indicators and the questions the dashboard must answer.
  • Your visual identity.
  • Who will update the data.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form, with the audience and the three questions the dashboard must answer.
  2. We check your account, licence and permissions.
  3. We send a wireframe of the dashboard for you to approve with the offer.
  4. We prepare and clean the data and publish the layers in your account.
  5. We build the map, then the dashboard and its indicators.
  6. We test on phone and desktop, and with an ordinary viewer account.
  7. One revision round.
  8. We write the update guide and try it with your administrator.
  9. We hand over every item, owned by your account. The acceptance period is 7 days.

Accuracy and limits statement

The dashboard shows the data supplied by the client up to the last update date it displays. Its accuracy is that of the source data. It is not an official data source. Its continuity depends on the client's subscription and account permissions.

Out of scope

  • Buying licences and subscriptions
  • ArcGIS Enterprise and its administration
  • Mobile apps
  • Hosting by Madar
  • Integration with enterprise systems
  • Periodic updates without a contract
