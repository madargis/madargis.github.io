GIS Training and Coaching

A one-to-one coaching session or a live workshop for a small team, on an example close to its own work, with open data.

From USD 300 per workshopDelivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

Up-to-date Arabic material on Python, data QA and remote sensing in a local context is scarce. New teams learn by trial and error on delivery data.

Who it is for

New GIS teams at offices and consultancies, organisations asking for capacity building, and job seekers, learners and postgraduate students.

A starting price. The final price is written in a one-page offer.

What you receive

  • A one-hour one-to-one coaching session with a written summary and next steps.
  • Or a live workshop for a small team, 3 to 6 hours, on an example close to its work.
  • Exercise material: open data, scripts, a checklist.
  • A recording of the session, if everyone agrees.

What you supply

  • The trainees' level.
  • The software and versions they have.
  • The practical goal of the training.
  • An example of their kind of work, with no confidential data.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form: who the trainee is, their level and what they want to be able to do afterwards.
  2. A 15-minute call to fix the practical goal.
  3. We send a one-page offer: topics, duration, outputs, price and hardware requirements.
  4. We prepare the exercise on open data and test the installation on a clean machine.
  5. We send installation instructions and the data three days before the session.
  6. We run the session: a short explanation, then practice the trainee does personally.
  7. We send the summary, the material and the recording within two days.
  8. A follow-up a week later with one question about applying it at work.

Accuracy and limits statement

Practical training on tools and method. It grants no accredited professional certificate and no licence, and it guarantees neither a job nor acceptance of the trainee's outputs by any authority. Examples are built on open data.

Out of scope

  • Accredited certificates
  • Software licences
  • Doing the trainee's work for them
  • Solving assignments or writing theses
  • Training on hydrological or traffic modelling

A request form with fixed fields. Do not send data yet.
