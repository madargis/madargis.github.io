Survey Data Drafting

Turning the surveyor's points into a CAD drawing or GIS layers: features, contours, coordinate tables and the office template.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

The field team collects faster than the office can draw, so drawings fall behind.

Who it is for

Survey and engineering offices at peak times, such as Real Estate Registry batches, and contractors with surveys that were never drawn.

What you receive

  • The DWG drawing with layers in the office template.
  • Coordinate and area tables.
  • A GIS copy, if requested.

What you supply

  • The point file with codes.
  • The field sketch.
  • The office template and standards.

How the work goes

  1. We agree codes and template on a sample file.
  2. We draw features and contours.
  3. We check the drawing against the sketch and send questions.
  4. We hand the drawing to the engineer for review and signature.

Accuracy and limits statement

The drawing follows the points and sketch as received. No judgement on observation quality. It becomes an official deliverable only after review and signature by the licensed office.

Out of scope

  • Field observation
  • Survey decision and signature
  • Uploading to the Balady platform

Tools

  • AutoCAD
  • Civil 3D
  • QGIS
  • Excel
