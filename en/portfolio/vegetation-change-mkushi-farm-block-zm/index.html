Vegetation change in Mkushi farm block, Central Province, Zambia: Sentinel-2 NDVI, 2018 to 2025

Comparing the July to August seasons of 2018 and 2025, NDVI fell by 0.2 or more on 3,949 ha and rose by 0.2 or more on 4,954 ha of the 91,551 ha analysed. Two single scenes of the 2018 season, compared the same way, differ on 651 ha, which is the noise level of the method.

The problem

The Mkushi farm block is one of Zambia's main commercial farming areas, with winter wheat grown under centre pivots. The question is where green cover was lost or gained between 2018 and 2025, how much, and how sure the answer is, using only open imagery.

The data

Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance (Copernicus programme), bands B04, B08 and the scene classification layer, plus B02 and B03 for the true colour, read as windows of cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs through the Earth Search STAC API (Element 84, collection sentinel-2-l2a) on 21 November 2026: S2A_35LQE_20180808_1_L2A, S2B_35LQE_20180813_1_L2A, S2B_35LQE_20180823_1_L2A, S2A_35LQE_20250724_0_L2A, S2B_35LQE_20250806_0_L2A, S2B_35LQE_20250826_0_L2A. Attribution as the Copernicus Sentinel legal notice requires: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2018, 2025. Locator: Natural Earth, public domain.

The method

  1. Picked up to 3 of the clearest scenes in July to August of 2018 (3) and of 2025 (3) for a 33 × 28 km window, at 20 m in EPSG:32735.
  2. Masked no-data, cloud, cirrus and cloud shadow with the scene classification layer and took the per-pixel median NDVI = (B08 − B04) / (B08 + B04) of each season.
  3. Change = NDVI 2025 minus NDVI 2018: loss at −0.2 or less, gain at +0.2 or more, strong beyond ±0.4; patches under 9 pixels dropped; recounted at ±0.15 and ±0.25.
  4. Noise: the same rule applied to two single scenes of the 2018 season, scaled to the analysed area.

The result

Analysed 91,551 ha (100% of the window clear on both dates). NDVI loss 3,949 ha (strong 2,957 ha), gain 4,954 ha (strong 3,070 ha); at ±0.15: loss 4,766 and gain 7,678 ha, at ±0.25: loss 3,554 and gain 4,011 ha. Land with NDVI of 0.4 or more: 30,265 ha in 2018 and 37,451 ha in 2025; median NDVI 0.32 then 0.35. Two single scenes of the 2018 season, compared the same way, differ on 651 ha, which is the noise level of the method.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. NDVI measures greenness, not land use, crop type, tree species or ownership: a harvested field, a dry season, drought or a fire scar can all read as loss, and irrigation or a wet year as gain. Two seasons are compared, not a trend. At 10 to 20 m, small plots, hedges and single trees are mixed with their surroundings. Cloud masks are imperfect. The map shows where to look, not a verified cause; field checks or higher-resolution imagery confirm it.
