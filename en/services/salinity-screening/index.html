Soil Salinity, Sabkha and Surface Water Quality Screening from Imagery

First maps of salt-affected soils and sabkhas, and turbidity indices in lakes, calibrated with field measurements supplied by the client.

Quoted per scopeIn preparation

This service is in preparation and cannot be requested yet.

The problem

An organisation needs to see the extent and change of salinity over a large area, and field samples are sparse.

Who it is for

Farms, soil and water consultants, development organisations and researchers.

What you receive

  • Salinity and sabkha index maps.
  • The index relationship to field measurements, where available.
  • A report with method and limits.

What you supply

  • The area boundary.
  • Soil or water electrical conductivity readings with coordinates and dates.

How the work goes

  1. You fill in the request form.
  2. We check the imagery and field readings.
  3. We compute the indices and calibrate them against the readings.
  4. We deliver the maps and report.

Accuracy and limits statement

In preparation: Madar has no documented practice yet in calibrating salinity or water-quality indices. When available: indices measure the surface only, not groundwater salinity, and without field readings the result is relative.

Out of scope

  • Sampling and laboratory analysis
  • Groundwater salinity
  • Reclamation recommendations
