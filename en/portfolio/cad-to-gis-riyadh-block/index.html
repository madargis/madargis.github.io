CAD to GIS and Ain el Abd to KSA-GRF17 transformation, a Riyadh block

We read a layered DXF in Ain el Abd and rebuilt it as attributed GIS layers in a KSA-GRF17 GeoPackage. Relabelling the coordinates instead of transforming them moves every vertex by 75.7 m.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

75.67mshift between the correct transformation and a relabelled code

Demo location

CAD to GIS Conversion and Coordinate Transformation

The problem

Legacy CAD drawings arrive as lines and text with no attribute tables, in Ain el Abd. Authorities ask for GIS layers in KSA-GRF17. A common mistake is to give the coordinates the new system's name without transforming them.

The data

No open CAD drawing exists, so we made a stand-in: an OpenStreetMap extract (roads, buildings and land use in a 920 × 786 m box near Kingdom Centre, Riyadh) exported to a layered DXF in Ain el Abd / UTM 38N (EPSG:20438) with operation EPSG:1111 (stated accuracy 18 m). Downloaded through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20. Licence: ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The method

  1. Export the stand-in to DXF: one layer per road class and land use, one building layer, street names as text. Result: 18 layers.
  2. Read the DXF with ezdxf, turn each closed polyline into its own polygon, and take the class from the layer name.
  3. Join 89 street-name texts to the nearest road line, and compute length, area and geometry validity.
  4. Transform to KSA-GRF17 / UTM 38N (EPSG:9358) with the pyproj operation Ain el Abd to KSA-GRF17 (1) (EPSG:9362).
  5. Compare every vertex with a copy given the EPSG:9358 code without transformation, and measure the shift in metres.

The result

A 3-layer GeoPackage: 176 road lines, 284 buildings, 8 land-use polygons, from 468 source features. 89 road lines took their street name from the CAD text. Invalid geometry: 0 (0 in the source). Open polylines on polygon layers: 0. Buildings inside another building, kept as separate polygons: 1. pyproj operation: Ain el Abd to KSA-GRF17 (1) (EPSG:9362), stated accuracy 2 m. Shift between correct transformation and relabelling: mean 75.67 m (range 75.65 to 75.69 m), bearing 275°, over 2,722 vertices. Round-trip difference from the original OpenStreetMap coordinates: 11.4 m.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. The DXF is a clean stand-in made from OpenStreetMap, so it does not show the problems of real CAD drawings such as unclosed polylines, blocks and mixed layers. A closed polyline inside another is read as a separate polygon, not a hole; that is right here because the source has no holes, and may be wrong for another drawing. The more accurate operation EPSG:9363 (ARAMCO grid, 5 to 10 cm) is not available to pyproj on this machine, so the result is good to about 2 m. The 11.4 m round-trip difference comes from the two published operations EPSG:1111 and EPSG:9362 disagreeing, not from the method. No ground check points. Not an official survey deliverable.
