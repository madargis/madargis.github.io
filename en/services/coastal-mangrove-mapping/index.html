Coastline and Mangrove Mapping

Mapping shoreline change and the extent of mangroves and sabkha over the years, for environmental and tourism studies.

Quoted per scope

Delivered by Madar

Request this service

The problem

A coastal project needs a documented baseline of the shore before construction.

Who it is for

Environmental consultants on coastal projects, resort developers and researchers.

What you receive

  • Shorelines per year and change rates.
  • A mangrove extent map and area.
  • A method and accuracy note.

What you supply

  • The coastal area boundary.
  • The years required.

How the work goes

  1. We collect scenes at similar tide states where possible.
  2. We extract shoreline and mangroves.
  3. We compute change and review a sample.
  4. We deliver maps and tables.

Accuracy and limits statement

10 to 30 m resolution, and tides shift the line. It is not an environmental impact assessment or an official maritime boundary.

Out of scope

  • Hydrographic survey
  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Field verification

Tools

  • Google Earth Engine
  • Sentinel-2
  • Landsat
  • QGIS
