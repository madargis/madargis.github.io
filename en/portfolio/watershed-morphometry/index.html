Morphometry of the Wadi Na'man catchment east of Makkah from NASADEM

We delineated the Wadi Na'man catchment above an outlet where it leaves the mountains, ordered its streams by Strahler and computed its shape parameters. It covers 661 km², reaches order 5 and has a drainage density of 0.82 km/km².

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

Demo date2026-11-20

Related serviceWatershed Delineation and Morphometric Analysis

The problem

A researcher, agency or farm wants a first picture of a wadi catchment: its boundary, network, stream orders and a table of shape parameters, with a stated source and accuracy, before any study.

The data

NASADEM_HGT version 001 by NASA JPL (2020): 1 arc-second (about 30 m) elevations from the SRTM flight of February 2000, in whole metres above the EGM96 geoid, tiles n21e039 and n21e040. Only a window was read from Microsoft Planetary Computer (collection nasadem, no account) on 20 November 2026. Licence: openly shared without restriction under the NASA EOSDIS guidance. Citation: NASA JPL. (2020). NASADEM Merged DEM Global 1 arc second V001 [Data set]. NASA Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center. https://doi.org/10.5067/MEASURES/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001. Wadi name: the waterway named وادي نعمان in OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on the same day, © OpenStreetMap contributors, licence ODbL.

The method

  1. Read a 65 × 65 km window (EPSG:32637, x 595000 to 660000, y 2325000 to 2390000) and reprojected it to 30 m cells.
  2. Filled depressions by priority flood (Wang and Liu 2006) and derived D8 flow direction and accumulation.
  3. Outlet on the OpenStreetMap line of Wadi Na'man at 40.0320° E, 21.3341° N, moved downstream to the first cell draining 100 km²; delineated the catchment and checked that it does not touch the window edge.
  4. Streams: cells draining 1 km² or more, ordered by Strahler, recomputed at 0.5 and 2 km².
  5. Basin length: straight distance from the outlet to the farthest point of the catchment. Perimeter: the boundary simplified by one cell (30 m).
  6. Computed bifurcation ratio, drainage density, stream frequency, form factor, elongation ratio, circularity ratio, relief, relief ratio, and the hypsometric curve and integral.

The result

Area 661 km², perimeter 149 km, basin length 30.8 km and longest flow path 59.5 km. Elevation runs from 336 m at the outlet to 2,604 m, a relief of 2,267 m and a relief ratio of 0.074; mean slope is 19.2°. The highest order is 5, with 187, 44, 12, 3 and 1 streams of orders 1 to 5 and 542 km of channel in total. Mean bifurcation ratio 3.73, drainage density 0.82 km/km², stream frequency 0.37 per km². Form factor 0.70, elongation ratio 0.94, circularity ratio 0.37 and hypsometric integral 0.27.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. These are shape parameters, not discharges, flood levels or a hydrology study, and they are not for plan approval. The NASADEM user guide (NASA JPL, 2020) gives the SRTM requirement as 16 m absolute vertical error at 90% and a global average absolute height error of about 6.8 m; the model is a radar surface of February 2000 in whole metres. The network and its density follow the threshold: at 0.5 km² the highest order becomes 6 and the density 1.11 km/km²; at 2 km² the order is 4 and the density 0.61. Shape ratios follow the length definition: with the longest flow path as the length, the form factor would be 0.19. A boundary drawn from 30 m cells is longer than the real one; before simplification the perimeter is 187 km. The outlet and boundary must be checked against recent imagery and a field survey, and dams and roads built after 2000 are not represented.
