About Madar

A small digital office that delivers geospatial services remotely and builds tools that shorten everyday GIS work.

What Madar is

Madar is a small digital office that delivers geospatial services remotely and builds tools that shorten everyday GIS work.

It is led by a GIS specialist with a BSc in Geomatics and Surveying Engineering and the PMP certification.

Who we work for

  • Organisations: engineering and survey offices, consultants, real estate developers, contractors on government programmes, development organisations.
  • Individuals: landowners and small investors, researchers and postgraduate students, GIS specialists who want tools or training.

How we work

  1. You fill in the request form with fixed fields. No data is sent at this step.
  2. We reply with one of three outcomes: it fits, it needs a licensed office to sign, or it is outside our scope.
  3. We ask a few short questions and request a small data sample.
  4. We send a one-page offer: scope, deliverables, exclusions, price, schedule, acceptance criterion. It is valid for 14 days.
  5. Both parties sign the scope of work before any work starts.
  6. We work on a copy of the data. Every change of scope needs a written note.
  7. We deliver one package: the deliverables, metadata, the accuracy statement, the limits of use and the method note.
  8. A 7-day acceptance period. Anything that does not meet the written acceptance criterion is corrected.

Working rules

  • Your original data is kept as a read-only copy, and work happens on a second copy.
  • Client data never enters the portfolio, the lessons or the test data of the tools.
  • Client data is not uploaded to a third party, and it is deleted after a period written in the scope of work.
  • No work before a written scope, and every change by written note.
  • The numbers in reports are computed by code.
  • QGIS, Python and GDAL are open source. Commercial software runs on the client's licence inside the client's environment, or on a licence Madar owns.
  • Open data is cited with its source and licence in every deliverable: OpenStreetMap under ODbL, Copernicus and Landsat.

What we commit to

  • Every demo in the portfolio is a demo built from open data, not a client project.
  • There are no client logos, no client testimonials, and no project counts or years-of-experience figures on this site.
  • We claim no licence, accreditation or partnership.
  • For hydrology studies, traffic impact studies and official survey deliverables, Madar provides spatial support only; signing belongs to a licensed office.
  • Every deliverable comes with an accuracy and limits statement.
  • We do not guarantee that any authority will accept a deliverable.
