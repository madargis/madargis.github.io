AlUla heritage and visitor map with a bilingual site list

We merged 71 heritage and visitor sites from Wikidata and OpenStreetMap into one numbered list, 23 of them with both Arabic and English names, plus visitor services and a 59 km driving route linking five main sites.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

The problem

Heritage site information is spread across sources in different languages, with no single database or map for visitors or operators.

The data

Wikidata (licence CC0) through its SPARQL service: every item with coordinates within 30 km of central AlUla and inside the map box, downloaded 2026-11-20. OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20: historic and tourism features, visitor services and the road network in the box 26.5 to 26.9 N, 37.78 to 38.08 E. Licence ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The method

  1. Of 207 Wikidata items around AlUla we kept 22 with a heritage designation (P1435) or a heritage type: archaeological site, ancient city, tomb, museum, castle, petroglyph, rock formation, Hejaz Railway station.
  2. From OpenStreetMap we took features tagged historic, heritage, or tourism as attraction, museum, viewpoint or gallery.
  3. Sources are linked only by the wikidata tag written in OpenStreetMap, with no fuzzy name matching; any two sites 300 m or less apart are flagged for review.
  4. Arabic and English names come from Wikidata labels, then OpenStreetMap name:ar, name:en and name when written in Arabic or Latin script; what is still missing stays blank.
  5. Sites are numbered north to south; visitor services are hotels, guest houses, camps, information points and toilets.
  6. Driving route: shortest path on the OpenStreetMap road network from the Old Town to Dadan, Jabal Ikmah, Elephant Rock and Hegra.

The result

The list has 71 sites: 6 in both sources, 15 in Wikidata only, 50 in OpenStreetMap only. 23 have both names, 9 Arabic only, 39 English only. Mapped visitor services: 28. 142 unnamed features were left out. 51 sites have another listed site within 300 m and are flagged for review, not merged. Route: AlUla Old Town to Dadan 1.2 km, Dadan to Jabal Ikmah 15.7 km, Jabal Ikmah to Elephant Rock 16.1 km, Elephant Rock to Hegra 25.6 km; the farthest stop is 1108 m from the nearest mapped road.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. Not archaeological documentation or a field survey. Sites and their positions follow what contributors entered in Wikidata and OpenStreetMap, and some coordinates are one point for a large site. The list is not a complete inventory of AlUla, and some features contributors tagged as attractions are visitor facilities such as bus stops. Types and names were not checked in the field, and we did not write missing translations ourselves. The route is the shortest path on the mapped network and ignores opening hours, tickets and closed roads. In a real project, coordinates of sensitive sites are not published.
