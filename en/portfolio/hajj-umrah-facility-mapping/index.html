Camps, facilities and routes in Mina as mapped in OpenStreetMap

We mapped 566 camps, 52 health points and nearby services in Mina and measured walking along the network: 87% of camps are within 10 minutes of a mapped health point. The map shows what is mapped, not everything that exists.

A demo built from open data, not a client project.

The problem

Pilgrim service companies need an up-to-date map of camps, facilities and the distances between them every season; many are drawn by hand or spread across files.

The data

OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API on 2026-11-20: camps, hospitals, clinics, toilets, civil defence, information points, Mashaer metro stops, the Jamarat Bridge and the walking network in the box 21.4 to 21.435 N, 39.855 to 39.915 E. Licence ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors. No aerial imagery.

The method

  1. Classify features by their tags: tourism=camp_site for camps, amenity or healthcare for hospitals and clinics, amenity=toilets and fire_station, tourism=information, railway for stops.
  2. OpenStreetMap walking network of paths and streets at 4.8 km/h: 5 min = 400 m and 10 min = 800 m.
  3. For each camp: shortest walking distance from a point inside it to the nearest mapped hospital or clinic, and to the Jamarat Bridge.
  4. Completeness: count per feature type, share with a name, and median year of the last edit.

The result

Camps 566: within 5 min of a health point 290, 5 to 10 min 204, over 10 min 72. Median walk to the nearest health point 395 m, to the Jamarat Bridge 3.0 km (farthest camp 4.9 km). Mapped: 11 hospitals, 41 clinics, 53 toilets, 43 civil defence or fire stations. 99% of camps carry a name; 257 more features are named as camps under another tag.

Limits

Demonstration on open data, not a client project. OpenStreetMap completeness in Mina is unknown: there is no open reference to measure it against, so counts describe what is mapped, not what exists, and toilets and medical points inside camps are mostly not mapped. Camp outlines and numbers may not match the approved plan for the season. Walking distance on the mapped network ignores crowding, closed routes and movement schedules; this is not crowd analysis or a safety plan. A real project builds the map from the client's approved plans.
